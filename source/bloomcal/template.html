Why a calendar

Timing is the actual question — people know they want flowers, they don't know when. Making the year the interface answers it before anyone reads a word.

The front really moves

Pin positions are real coordinates and the band follows the degree-day model: north through spring, back south through autumn as the leaves turn.

Built to be shared

A scrubable year is screenshot-able and embeddable in a way a filter grid never is — which is how a new domain earns the links it needs to rank.

The tool is still there

Scroll past the year and it becomes a conventional search-and-grid site, so visitors who arrive knowing what they want aren't made to play with a toy.
