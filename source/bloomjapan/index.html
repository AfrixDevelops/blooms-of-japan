Palette

Cream ground, deep plum text, coral for every primary action, sage for confirmation, lilac for quiet accents. Light mode only — no pure white and no pure black anywhere, so it stays soft on the eyes.

Map demoted to a view

A national map answers "where in Japan is this?", not "what's near me" — so it's now a List/Map toggle on Home that inherits your filters, and the freed tab went to trip planning instead.

Built for visitors

Each spot names its area the way a visitor thinks of it ("Tokyo Area", "Kyoto & Kansai"), carries its Japanese name to show station staff, and leads with the nearest train station rather than a street address.

Try it

Walk the onboarding, then switch Home between List and Map — the map inherits whatever you've filtered to, and tapping a pin opens its callout. The Trip tab takes dates and a base city.
